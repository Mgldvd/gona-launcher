# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first public version.

**Tiles.** Apps live in tiles you split right or down, name, colour and drag between. Icons shrink so none is ever cut
off. Each tile's ＋ and ⋯ buttons show after the pointer has rested on it (or never), and a right-click opens its menu.

**Ready-made profiles.** The first start offers Omarchy, Top, Dock, Focus and Clean; later they are added as profiles next
to yours. Profiles switch with Ctrl+P, buttons in the strip or a keybinding, in the order they were made.

**Opening.** A centred window, a panel on any of the four edges, or full screen, with twenty opening effects (Fluid, Genie,
Bounce, Matrix, Fireworks, Blackhole, Beams and more) and an option to reduce the motion.

**Finding apps.** Type to filter the tiles, or every installed app; the most used (or most recent) apps show in a row.
**Ctrl+A** shows every app; **?** lists the keyboard shortcuts.

**The bar button.** Left click toggles, middle click opens all apps, right click opens a small menu: Settings, Reload, About
and the version. **Super** alone opens the launcher.

**Yours.** Dark and Light, following the Omarchy theme, every colour editable; one plain `config.toml`, export / import,
and Reset to factory settings (it keeps a copy of everything).
